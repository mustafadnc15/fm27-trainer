## FM27 Trainer 0.2.9 — custom training schedules, including shared .fmf files

### New
- **Use training schedules people share online.** On the Training page, *Custom schedules* → *Import .fmf…*
  (or drop the files on the app window) reads the `.fmf` that FM27 exports from its training screen. You can
  also build a schedule yourself, or save any coming week as FM has it.
- **Put them on the coming weeks.** Give a schedule to the next few weeks (*Use for N weeks*), to one week (the
  dropdown on each week card), or make it the default for every week. The app writes it into FM's calendar
  instead of its own plan and, with *Keep training on plan automatically* on, puts it back when FM's assistant
  re-plans a week.
- **Repeating pattern.** Switch on *Repeating pattern* and list schedules in order, for example C1, C2, C3, C4:
  each week gets the next one, then it starts again. A week's own choice goes before the pattern, the pattern
  goes before the default schedule.
- Match and travel days always stay with FM, and a slot set to *FM's choice* is left alone. Per schedule you can
  let the days around a match follow the app's own rhythm (recovery after, match preparation before), so a
  schedule built for a weekend match still fits a week with a midweek fixture.

### Good to know
- The `.fmf` reader was checked on five shared FM27 schedules. The first day of a shared schedule is taken as
  Monday.
- **Not yet tested live in FM.** Writing to FM's calendar works the same way as the automatic plan, but this
  feature has not been tried on a real save yet. Use a test save first and check the weeks in FM's training
  calendar.
